Creatures

Retail's creatures, live from wago.tools: find one, look at it in 3D, add it with +, then Install. Your client reads the models as retail made them - it needs WarcraftXL with the wxl.modern-m2 mod - and Install adds the creature to your client, your server's tables and your world database.

Spells

        
Retail's spells, live from wago.tools: open one to watch its visuals as the game plays them -
          every model each moment of the cast puts on the caster, the target or the ground, with retail's timing.
          Make a new spell from one: it gets the retail spell's name, text, icon, timing and visual, and the mechanics of a
          3.3.5 spell you pick; Install adds it to your client and server, and .learn <id> teaches it
          (the client needs WarcraftXL with wxl.modern-m2).

Item stats

        
The backport kit makes every piece a level-1 cosmetic copy with no stats. Give them real
          ones here: set what all pieces share, fill in the stats, then adjust any piece - the tooltip shows it as
          it will look in game. Numbers follow WotLK's own items.

      

    

    

    

      

      

      

        

    

    

      
Item sets

        

          

      
A set's pieces show it in their tooltip, and wearing enough of them gives its bonuses - spells
        your 3.3.5 client already has (every bonus of its own item sets), or any spell by its id.
